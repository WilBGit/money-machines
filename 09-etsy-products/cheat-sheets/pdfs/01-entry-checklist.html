Entry Checklist — 10 Gate Checks Before Buying an Option

Rule: Score each check 0–2. Minimum 14/20 to enter. Below 12 = hard NO.

# Gate Check 2 pts 1 pt 0 pts
1 Trend Direction Strong trend in your direction Sideways/unclear Counter-trend
2 Volume Confirmation Volume > 50-day avg by 1.5× Volume at avg Below avg volume
3 RSI Zone Oversold (calls) / Overbought (puts) Neutral (40–60) Wrong side of RSI
4 SMA Alignment Price above 20 & 50 SMA (calls) Price above 1 SMA Price below key SMAs
5 Support/Resistance Clear level within 2% Zone nearby No clear level
6 IV Percentile IV < 30th percentile IV 30–60th pct IV > 60th pct
7 Catalyst Timing Earnings/event within 1–3 weeks Vague catalyst No catalyst
8 Risk/Reward R:R ≥ 3:1 R:R 2:1 R:R < 2:1
9 Position Size OK ≤ 2% of account per trade ≤ 5% of account > 5% of account
10 Daily Loss Room Under 50% daily loss limit At 75% of limit At or past limit

Scoring Guide

Score Action
18–20 🟢 Green light — High-conviction entry
14–17 🟡 Amber — Proceed with reduced size (50%)
12–13 🔴 Red — Wait for better setup
Below 12 ⛔ Hard NO — Do not trade

Quick Pre-Trade Mantra

1) Is the trend with me? 2) Is volume confirming? 3) Is IV reasonable? 4) Can I afford the loss?
