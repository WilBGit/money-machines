Trade Review Framework — 5 Post-Trade Questions

Complete this review within 24 hours of closing every trade. No exceptions.

The 5 Questions

# Question Why It Matters
1 Did I follow my entry checklist? Enforces discipline — most losses come from skipping checks
2 Was my thesis correct? Validates or invalidates your reasoning, not just the outcome
3 Did I respect my stop-loss? Slippage on stops is the #1 account killer
4 What would I do differently? Extracts the lesson — every trade teaches something
5 Was this within my edge? Confirms you traded your strategy, not your emotions

Metrics to Track (Minimum 30 trades)

Metric Formula Target
Win Rate Wins ÷ Total Trades ≥ 45%
Avg Win / Avg Loss Avg $win ÷ Avg $loss ≥ 1.5:1
Profit Factor Gross Profit ÷ Gross Loss ≥ 1.5
Expectancy (Win% × AvgWin) − (Loss% × AvgLoss) Positive per trade
Max Drawdown Peak-to-trough decline < 10% of account
R-Multiple P&L ÷ Initial Risk $ Track avg per trade

Common Review Findings & Fixes

Finding Fix
Skipping checklist when excited Force 2-minute cooldown before every entry
Moving stops lower after entry Set stop in trading platform, don't touch it
Taking trades outside your edge Write your edge on a card; read before trading
Revenge trading after a loss Enforce daily loss limit — no exceptions
Cutting winners too short Use trailing stop or partial profit at 1R, let rest ride

Weekly Review Habit

  1. Every trade → fill the review template above
  2. Every Friday → review all week's trades in a journal
  3. Every month → recalculate your metrics table above
  4. Every quarter → if metrics below target, reduce size by 50% until improved
